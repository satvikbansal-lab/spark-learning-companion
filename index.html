What your parent can see

Your parent set up Parent View. They can see the topics you study, your quiz results and hints you asked for, and safety alerts (just the kind of alert, never your messages). They can’t see your chats, your notes or My Thoughts. Your parent also chooses some settings, like whether Spark gives direct answers.
